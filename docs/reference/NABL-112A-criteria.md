# NABL 112-A Issue 01 (18-Dec-2024) — Specific Criteria for Medical Laboratories
# Plus NABL 112-B Issue 01 (18-Dec-2024, Amd 01 04-Nov-2025) — Guidance
# These were issued AFTER Noble's DI Issue IV (01-Apr-2024), so alignment must be checked explicitly.

## Structural (cl. 5)
- Lab Director full-time; part-time acceptable only for Micro/Mini/Small labs at minimum 4 hrs/day.
- Dedicated Quality Officer/Manager required.
- Risk management reviewed annually across ALL processes.

## Personnel (6.2)
- Large/Very Large labs: MD/DNB/DCP/PhD in the relevant discipline for EACH discipline.
- Medium labs: at least one full-time MD/DNB/DCP/PhD.
- Specialised tests need documented competence assessment + training records.
- Competence assessment format given in NABL 112-B (primary/secondary discipline A–J, job responsibilities in QC, interpretation, regulatory compliance; evidence of day-to-day involvement and troubleshooting).
- New staff assessed twice in first year (common NABL expectation).

## Facilities (6.3)
- Adequate space preventing cross-contamination; separate incompatible activities; fume hood for formalin/chemicals.
- Microbiology minimum BSL-2. TB culture/sensitivity requires BSL-3, Class II A2 cabinet, mechanical ventilation, negative pressure. Direct smear/CBNAAT only = adequately ventilated area.
- Mycology: dedicated physically separated area, Class II A2 cabinet, BOD incubator.
- Uninterrupted power supply.
- ELECTRICAL SAFETY VERIFICATION EVERY 6 MONTHS.
- Temperature-controlled equipment with 24/7 monitoring where critical.

## Equipment (6.4–6.5)
- IQ/OQ/PQ documented before use.
- Automated analysers calibrated per manufacturer spec; RAW DATA MANDATORY in calibration certificates.
- Carry-over checks on reagent changes.
- Spectrophotometers 6–12 month calibration interval.
- Non-analytical equipment calibrated BY NABL-ACCREDITED LABS: autoclave 1 yr, balances 1 yr, centrifuges 1 yr, pipettes 1 yr, thermometers 1 yr, thermocyclers 1 yr/manufacturer, mass weights 2–3 yrs (E1 class).

## Reagents (6.6)
- Grade Water-II for reconstitution, with documented water testing.
- Labels: expiry, storage requirement, preparation date. In-house reagents need preparer's signature + storage instructions.
- Lot verification (NABL 112-B): chemistry min 2 patient samples or QC on old AND new lot; coagulation correlation R >= 0.97; molecular reproducibility on prior-tested samples; criteria also given for stains, media, serology.

## Service agreements / referral (6.7–6.8)
- Users informed which tests are NOT accredited.
- Accredited scope with TAT provided to users.
- Referral permitted only for: second opinion (histopath, cytopath, cytogenetics, molecular), temporary equipment failure/disaster. Referral labs must be NABL-accredited or established institutions (NCDC, NIV, CCMB).
- Telepathology: risk analysis, traceability documentation, quality indicator monitoring, defined consultation guidelines.

## Pre-examination (7.2)
- Evacuated tube systems preferred; ORDER OF DRAW MANDATORY.
- Informed consent for HIV and invasive procedures.
- Sample transport risk assessment; 4–8 °C or frozen (dry ice for sensitive analytes); TRIPLE PACKAGING (primary/secondary/rigid outer).
- Competency assessment of external collection agencies documented.
- NABL 112-B: three CC types (lab-owned, management-controlled, franchisee). Phlebotomist training documented on methodology, hygiene, safety, waste disposal. INTERNAL AUDIT OF EACH COLLECTION CENTRE AT LEAST ONCE A YEAR. Only CCs declared to NABL may be claimed. CC audit findings must be discussed at management review.

## Examination (7.3)
- Verification: accuracy, precision, linearity, inter-instrument checks using patient samples where possible.
- Validation required when modifying a regulatory-cleared method (different matrix, collection method, temperature, reagent dilution).
- MU: from IQC data, MINIMUM 6 MONTHS; ±1.96 × %CV (approx ±2 × %CV); lab's OWN mean/SD, not manufacturer's.
- NABL 112-B: preferably use the HIGHEST %CV of IQC data from the previous six months.
- IQC (7.3.7.2): TWO LEVELS DAILY, or one level per 8-hour shift. Frequency based on risk assessment. LJ charts plotted daily and reviewed for trends/shifts. Monthly mean, SD, %CV. Out-of-control analysis with documented corrective action.
- EQA/PT (7.3.7.3): mandatory participation prior to accreditation; required on methodology/equipment change and scope extension; corrective actions documented.
- Alternative QA when no EQA: inter-lab sample exchange, IQC material comparison pools, split/blind testing by multiple personnel/methods/analysers, reference materials, clinical correlation, replicate testing.
- Comparability (7.3.7.4): two identical or different systems require comparability study AT LEAST TWICE YEARLY using 10–20 samples; Bland-Altman/regression documented.

## Post-examination (7.4)
- Critical limits established and communicated immediately.
- Authorised personnel signature/name on reports.
- Preliminary reports permitted 24/7 if daily IQC valid and technicians competent.
- Auto-verification: verified clinical decision rules, reports clearly marked "Auto verified", middleware manufacturer validation certificate, system reviewed ANNUALLY, rapid suspension mechanism and contingency plan.
- Sample storage: Biochemistry 1 day at 2–8 °C; CBC 24 h at 2–8 °C; coagulation 4 h RT, frozen plasma up to 1 yr at −80 °C; Hb electrophoresis 1 week at 2–8 °C or below −20 °C.
- Biomedical waste per latest rules.

## Data & information (7.6)
- Computerised/paper system with audit trail; patient confidentiality.
- HALF-YEARLY VERIFICATION of input/retrieval/formatting for MINIMUM 10 SAMPLE TYPES.
- Interface verification: analyser↔LIS and LIS↔HIS.
- Role-based authenticated access; documented user inactivation.
- Audit trail demonstrating user-data linkage.

## Records retention (8.4)
- Test request forms/reports: 1 month (NABL minimum; national rules may require longer)
- Molecular gel images/RT-PCR: 1 yr infectious / 10 yrs genetic & cancer
- Flow cytometry data: 5 yrs
- Electrophoretogram: 1 yr or next assessment
- Hb HPLC data: 2 yrs
- IQC raw data / LJ charts: 1 yr or next assessment
- Histopathology reports, blocks, slides: 5 yrs
- Cytopathology slides: 5 yrs
- Cytogenetics/FISH images: 5 yrs

## Evaluations (8.8)
- Quality indicators must cover: sample collection, transportation, analysis, reporting, TAT, complaints, downtime, measurement uncertainty, PT/EQA performance.
- Internal audit at least ANNUALLY covering pre-, examination, post-examination PLUS primary collection and POCT if applicable.

## Discipline specifics
### Clinical Biochemistry
- Hormone stimulation tests under medical supervision in hospital settings.
- Blood gas QC: one level every 8 hours.
- Specimen retention 1 day at 2–8 °C.
### Clinical Pathology
- Urine dipstick: documented criteria for erroneous results; DAILY positive/negative dipstick controls.
- Stool: RBC kit validation with each new box; Lugol's iodine weekly.
- Semen: latest WHO guidelines; split testing quarterly if no formal PT.
- Body fluids: processed immediately, 12 h preservation at 4 °C if delayed; 2-level QC for automated analysis.
### Haematology
- Anticoagulant therapy requests must state purpose; sample timing documented.
- 3.2% sodium citrate for coagulation.
- CBC checked for clots; processed within 8 h, max 24 h.
- RDW reported as SD or %CV.
- ESR: Westergren/ICSH/CLSI within 6 h (24 h at 4 °C).
- Manual Hb: minimum four calibration curve concentrations.
- Coagulation within 4 h; frozen plasma up to 1 yr at −80 °C.
- Platelet-poor plasma: 1500–2000 g for 15 min (<10,000/µl); VERIFICATION EVERY 6 MONTHS.
- PT report must include time, MNPT and INR; MNPT determined PER REAGENT LOT.
- Automated reticulocyte counts verified manually WEEKLY.
### Immunohaematology
- ABO by TWO different techniques (one with serum testing); cells confirmed for specificity per batch; reverse grouping with fresh cells.
- Weak D by indirect antiglobulin method if Rh(D) negative.
- Antibody screening: O pooled cells (validated/fresh) or commercial panels.
- DCT/ICT with positive and negative controls.
- Crossmatch across multiple phases.
### Microbiology & Serology
- BSL-2 design, biohazard signage.
- Dedicated media preparation room (laminar flow recommended).
- Autoclave/oven: CHEMICAL INDICATOR EACH BATCH, BIOLOGICAL INDICATOR WEEKLY.
- Transport media appropriate to specimen; immediate processing or refrigeration (except CSF, urethral/endocervical swabs, amoebic pus).
- AST per CLSI or EUCAST — edition must be current and stated.

## Process (NABL 100B, Issue 01 23-Nov-2022, Amd 03 27-Aug-2025)
- Renewal application at least 6 months before expiry.
- Reassessment within 24 months (preferably months 20–22) of grant/renewal; every 2 years.
- Annual surveillance onsite within 12 months (preferably month 10); may go 6-monthly on risk.
- Corrective action addressing ROOT CAUSE due within 30 DAYS of the assessment report.
- Accreditation validity extended to 4 years (NABL announcement 16-Oct-2024) with yearly surveillance and 2-yearly reassessment.
