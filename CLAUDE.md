## Why most findings exist

NABL 112-A and 112-B Issue 01 were published **18 December 2024** — eight months *after* NDC-DI Issue IV was issued. 112-B was amended again on 04-Nov-2025.

`NDC/DI/02` (normative references) lists external documents with only Name / Source / Responsibility / Mode — no issue number, issue date, amendment status or review step. It still carries "NABL 217 (As per ISO 15189:2012)" and a bare "NABL 112". NABL 112-A, 112-B and 100-B are absent entirely. That is the mechanism by which the new criteria were never picked up.

`NDC/DI/8.3` commits the lab to an annual document review. The Document Review and Revision Sheet has one row, dated 01-04-24, with the approval cell blank. Two annual reviews are missing.

**Consequence:** re-issuing the set as **Issue V**, dated 2026, with a revision-history entry naming NABL 112-A and 112-B Issue 01 as the trigger, is the highest-leverage single action available. It also answers the question the assessment team will ask at the opening meeting.

## Constraints and lead times

Items that **cannot** be closed in the final fortnight, because they need accumulated evidence or an external agency:

- EQAS/PT enrolment (Serology, Molecular, Clinical Pathology) — two cycles minimum
- Comparability studies — 10–20 samples, twice yearly, regression or Bland–Altman
- Half-yearly LIS verification on 10 sample **types** (currently one patient with 10 tests)
- Quality indicators for transportation, downtime and measurement uncertainty — six months of trend needed
- Electrical safety verification and all overdue calibrations — agency booking, peak season in September
- Continuous temperature logging with alarms — hardware lead time
- Internal audit (all departments **plus the CC**, against a checklist that includes NABL 112-A criteria) then MRM, both completed before the assessment; MRM at least 8 weeks ahead

## Decisions the lab owes itself

These are not paper fixes — someone has to choose:

1. **Dedicated Quality Manager?** NABL 112-A requires one. The post is currently held in addition to Lab Supervisor duties, and the documented qualification (MD/DNB) contradicts the post-holder (BSc MLT + MBA). Findings A-01, B-23.
2. **Lab Director's engagement and the lab's size category** — neither is stated anywhere, and the category drives the cl. 6.2 qualification rule. Findings A-02, B-24.
3. **The referral model.** ~30 tests are permanently outsourced. NABL 112-A permits referral only for second opinion (histopath, cytopath, cytogenetics, molecular) or temporary equipment failure. Findings A-03, B-01.
4. **Microbiology** — in scope with a named Microbiologist and declared biosafety level, or formally withdrawn. Findings A-14, B-21, B-24, C-33.

## The quality record platform (`app/`)

Next.js 16 + SQLite via `node:sqlite`, served on the laboratory network. Runs on
Node 22+ with no native modules and no database server. See `README.md`.

Reproduces **18 record formats** from DI 6 — all 7 in clause 6.3 and 11 in
clause 6.4 — as controlled copies matching the source pages, with the Noble
device and the two approval signatures in the footer. Navigation is organised by
sub-clause, and each format carries its "Page N of clause X" reference.

Record formats are declared as data in `app/src/lib/forms/specs/`. Adding the
remaining DI 6 formats, or DI 7 and DI 8, means writing a spec, not a component.

**When the set is re-issued as Issue V**, update the issue block in
`app/src/components/form/footer.tsx` — otherwise the platform keeps printing
Issue IV / 01 04 2024 / Rev 00 onto live records.

## House style for drafting

NDC documents run: **1 Policy / 2 Purpose / 3 Applicability / 4 Responsibility / 5 Procedure / 6 Forms and formats used for compliance / 7 Signages / 8 Reference documents and records**, with a footer carrying Doc No., "Page N of clause X", Issue No., Issue Date, Rev No. and Revision Date, and "CONTROLLED COPY" at the foot.

Applicability statements must name **both** WZ-409C and the collection centre at WZ-405A/A.
